Why it loads fast

A gallery this size usually crawls

Photographers lose bookings to slow sites, because the people browsing at 11pm on a phone do not wait. Every photograph here is served at the size it is actually displayed, in WebP, and nothing below the fold is fetched until it is needed.

Measuring this page load…

Each photograph is built at three widths. A phone downloads the 480px file, a laptop the 900px, and the full 1600px version is only fetched when a frame is opened. Below the fold nothing loads at all until you scroll to it, and each frame fades up from a 20-pixel placeholder that is embedded in the HTML, so there is no layout shift and no empty grey box.
